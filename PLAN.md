# AMAFH bilingual website — current scope

## Confirmed direction
Complete local English/Arabic website for AMAFH Commercial Brokers, a UAE commercial broker and service provider. Use the approved AMAFH pink/purple logo palette, Plus Jakarta Sans English headings, Manrope body text, Noto Sans Arabic, full RTL, the existing homepage service crawl and sticky footer reveal. Inner-page sections draw on the supplied GuideTax and Fundpro references. Domain confirmed: https://amafhdubai.ae.

## Delivery boundary
Local preview only. No deployment or backend. Default build stays noindex. Partner names, team profiles and social handles were not supplied; no invented affiliations, ratings, counts, approvals or rates. Product decisions remain with the provider. Enquiries are prepared locally and sent only when the visitor chooses an external app and sends the message.
