## Credit cards

**Search intent:** UAE credit card guidance; credit card requirements; compare card costs. **Suggested headline:** everyday spending, considered carefully.

**Original introduction:** Start with the way you actually use a card. Explore the costs, repayment conditions and benefits that matter to your routine, then ask AMAFH about the next steps for your enquiry.

**Content/checklist:** annual or joining fees; reward conditions; purchase interest/profit and cash-advance charges; repayment due dates. Have identification and income information ready for the provider's requested checklist. Eligibility differs by card and applicant.

**Practical FAQs:**
- Does paying the minimum avoid interest? Not necessarily. Paying only the minimum can increase the overall cost and the time needed to clear a balance. Review the issuer's repayment terms.
- What documents might be needed? Identification and evidence of income are common starting points; the issuer confirms its exact requirements.
- Is a card limit guaranteed? No. The issuing provider assesses the application and sets any approved limit.

Sources: [Emirates NBD card eligibility/documents](https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/help-and-support/eligibility-and-documents-for-credit-cards); [credit card Key Facts Statement](https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/kfs/credit-card). No bank-specific fee/rate copied into AMAFH content.

## Personal finance / personal loans

**Search intent:** personal finance UAE; personal loan support Dubai; required documents. **Suggested headline:** room for your plans, clarity on the commitment.

**Original introduction:** A monthly payment is only one part of a borrowing decision. Explore the amount, repayment period and full cost together, with space to consider your existing commitments.

**Content/checklist:** requested purpose and amount; regular income; current repayments; repayment term; processing/early-settlement costs; salary-transfer requirements. Typical provider documents may include Emirates ID, passport/residency records, salary evidence and recent statements. Self-employed applicants can face different requirements.

**Practical FAQs:**
- Is salary transfer always required? Requirements vary with the provider and product; confirm this before applying.
- Can self-employed people enquire? Yes; the provider determines availability and may request business and account records.
- What should I compare besides the instalment? Total repayments, interest/profit calculation, fees, term and early-settlement conditions.

Sources: [Emirates NBD personal loan application guidance](https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/help-and-support/apply-for-a-personal-loan); [CBUAE disclosure standards](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/rulebook/consumer-protection-standards). Do not generalize one bank's salary or employer criteria.

## Car loans

**Search intent:** car loan UAE; new and used car finance; auto loan documents. **Suggested headline:** your next drive, with a clearer plan.

**Original introduction:** Put the finance conversation alongside the vehicle search. Understanding your upfront contribution, ongoing payment and vehicle requirements can help you plan the purchase more confidently.

**Content/checklist:** vehicle price, new/used status and age; contribution; finance term and total cost; insurance and registration needs. A dealer quotation or vehicle details may be requested alongside identity, income and account records; used vehicles can require valuation or seller documents.

**Practical FAQs:**
- Can I enquire about a used car? Yes; availability depends on the lender's vehicle and applicant criteria.
- Will I need a dealer quotation? A quotation is commonly requested for new vehicles; the lender confirms the final checklist.
- Is finance approved before I choose a car? Ask the lender about its assessment stages. An enquiry itself does not confirm approval or a finance amount.

Source: [Emirates NBD auto-loan application guidance](https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/help-and-support/apply-for-an-auto-loan). Avoid universal down-payment percentages and turnaround times.

## Mortgage loans

**Search intent:** mortgage support Dubai; UAE home finance; mortgage pre-approval. **Suggested headline:** a place to call yours, a plan to get there.

**Original introduction:** Bring the property, deposit and long-term monthly commitment into one conversation. Explore the questions that matter before you move from searching to making a financing decision.

**Content/checklist:** target property and purpose; deposit and transaction budget; fixed/variable pricing and later rate changes; valuation, processing and settlement costs. Provider assessment may require identity, income/bank records and property information.

**Practical FAQs:**
- Is pre-approval final approval? No. Further steps can include property selection, valuation, documents and the lender's final decision.
- What should I budget besides the deposit? Ask for the full breakdown of lender, valuation, registration and other applicable purchase costs.
- Can repayments change? Variable pricing and fixed periods ending can change the applicable rate; read the offer's calculation and reset terms.

Sources: [ADCB mortgage products](https://www.adcb.com/en/personal/loans/home-loans/mortgage-loan); [ADCB Dream Home process/FAQs](https://www.adcb.com/en/Multimedia/PDFS/2025/june/adcb-dream-home-faqs.pdf); [standard mortgage information](https://www.adcb.com/en/personal/loans/home-loans/standard-mortgage-loan.aspx). Do not copy provider-specific LTV or fee promotions.

## Car insurance

**Search intent:** car insurance UAE; comprehensive vs third-party; motor insurance renewal. **Suggested headline:** cover for the road ahead.

**Original introduction:** The right conversation begins with what a policy covers, what it excludes and what you would pay when claiming. Share your vehicle and renewal needs with AMAFH to explore the next steps.

**Content/checklist:** third-party versus own-vehicle damage cover; excess; agency/garage repair terms; optional assistance and geographical cover; exclusions. Typical preparation: Emirates ID, driving licence, vehicle registration/details and any existing-policy or no-claims information.

**Practical FAQs:**
- Does third-party insurance repair my own car? Generally it addresses covered liability to others; it does not provide own-vehicle damage cover.
- Does comprehensive mean every event is covered? No. Policy conditions, exclusions and any optional benefits still matter.
- Is the cheapest premium the only comparison? Compare the excess, repairs, scope and exclusions as well as price.

Sources: [GIG Gulf cover comparison](https://www.giggulf.ae/en/blog/third-party-vs-comprehensive-car-insurance-what-uae-residents-need-to-know); [GIG Dubai motor guide/documents](https://www.giggulf.ae/en/blog/car-insurance-in-dubai-complete-guide-2025). Do not promise flood, regional, off-road or agency cover without the actual policy.

## Life insurance

**Search intent:** life insurance UAE; family protection; term life cover. **Suggested headline:** thoughtful protection for the people who matter.

**Original introduction:** Begin with the commitments your family depends on: household costs, outstanding borrowing and future plans. That conversation helps frame the cover amount and period you want to explore.

**Content/checklist:** protection amount and duration; affordable premiums; existing cover; beneficiary information; accurate health/lifestyle disclosure; exclusions and optional benefits. Provider assessment determines price, terms and any medical requirements.

**Practical FAQs:**
- What is term life cover? Protection for a defined period, with payment on a covered death during that period under policy terms.
- Is critical illness automatically included? No; check whether it is included, optional or a separate policy, and which definitions apply.
- Will a medical assessment be required? This depends on the insurer, cover and application information. Provide complete and accurate disclosures.

Sources: [Zurich life-insurance overview](https://www.zurich.ae/life-insurance); [Zurich life-insurance explanation](https://www.zurich.ae/learn-about-life-insurance). Avoid formulaic cover recommendations, investment-return promises or an assurance that every health condition is accepted.

## Current accounts

**Search intent:** current account UAE; salary account requirements; current account support Dubai. **Suggested headline:** everyday banking, considered around you.

**Original introduction:** Think about how money moves through your month. Salary payments, bills, transfers and access to cash can help you identify the account features worth discussing.

**Content/checklist:** salary-transfer conditions or average-balance requirements; account and transaction fees; debit/cheque facilities where available; digital access; residency and identification requirements. A bank may request Emirates ID, passport and income/address records; the exact checklist depends on the account and applicant.

**Practical FAQs:**
- Does every current account require salary transfer? No single condition applies to every account. Confirm the selected product's criteria and fees.
- Does AMAFH open or hold my account? The bank opens and operates the account after its checks. AMAFH supports the enquiry process.
- Are cheque books always included? Facilities depend on the bank, account and eligibility.

Source: [Emirates NBD standard current account](https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/accounts/current-accounts/standard-current-account). Do not promise free accounts or universal non-resident eligibility.

## Savings accounts

**Search intent:** savings account UAE; savings account access and requirements. **Suggested headline:** space for what you are saving towards.

**Original introduction:** Your savings account should make sense for both your goal and your need for access. Look at how returns are calculated, when they are paid and which conditions apply to your balance.

**Content/checklist:** minimum/average balances; balance tiers; interest/profit calculation and payment frequency; currency; withdrawal and fee conditions; opening documents. Products can calculate returns on different balance bases and apply conditions that affect the amount earned.

**Practical FAQs:**
- Is the advertised rate earned on every balance? Not necessarily. Tiers, qualifying balances and other conditions can affect the return.
- Can I access savings whenever I want? Access and withdrawal conditions vary by product; check them against your needs.
- Is a savings account the same as a fixed deposit? No. They are different product types with different access and term conditions; ask the provider to explain the selected account.

Sources: [Emirates NBD accounts Key Facts Statement](https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/kfs/cash); [opening savings/current accounts](https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/help-and-support/open-an-account); [fixed-deposit support](https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/help-and-support/open-a-fixed-deposit). Do not hardcode a headline savings rate or describe returns as universally guaranteed.
